# AntiTrade Product Video — Design System

## 1. Creative Direction

A precise, dark-mode product film for an independent trader's daily operating system. Real AntiTrade screens remain the hero. Motion should feel deliberate and tool-like: clean viewport reveals, measured zooms, thin signal lines, and warm copper transitions. The result should look like a credible trading workspace, not a speculative finance advertisement.

## 2. Color System

- Canvas: `#111111`
- Product base: `#181818`
- Product surface: `#1d1d1d`
- Primary text: `#f3f3f3`
- Muted text: `#a7a7a7`
- Border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`
- Copper accent: `#b88352`
- Accent highlight: `#d9a778`
- Positive: `#299764`
- Risk: `#d05e4d`

## 3. Typography

- Primary: `"SF Pro Text", "Helvetica Neue", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`
- Data and labels: `"SF Mono", "Roboto Mono", monospace`
- Headlines: 70–94px, weight 720–780, tight but not condensed
- Supporting copy: 26–34px, regular weight
- UI labels: 18–22px, uppercase tracking only for eyebrow text

## 4. Composition

- Format: `1920 × 1080`, 30 fps, landscape
- Safe margins: 96px horizontal, 72px vertical
- Real product captures sit inside a restrained desktop frame with a 14px radius
- Titles anchor to the upper-left; product UI fills most of the frame
- One clear idea per scene, with no more than two floating callouts
- Keep interface text readable; do not crop the primary KPI or navigation context

## 5. Motion Language

- Product frames enter with 24–48px travel, opacity, and a small scale settle
- Screenshot interiors receive a 2–3% deterministic Ken Burns zoom
- Copper wipes bridge major workflow steps
- Thin rules draw left-to-right; KPI chips rise with a short stagger
- Ease with `power3.out`, `expo.out`, and `power3.inOut`
- No perpetual loops, bounce-heavy movement, or flashy trading effects

## 6. Guardrails

- Dark mode only
- Use captured AntiTrade screens; do not recreate fake dashboards
- No cold-blue, purple, neon, or multicolor finance gradients
- No decorative blob/orb backgrounds
- Do not imply guaranteed returns, execution, or investment advice
- Keep all claims tied to visible product capabilities
