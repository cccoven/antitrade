# AntiTrade Product Tour

## Goal

Show how AntiTrade connects market research, AI-assisted review, automation, journaling, strategy work, and portfolio context in one self-hosted workspace.

## Audience

Independent traders and technically capable users evaluating AntiTrade from GitHub.

## Voice

Warm, calm, and credible. Product-led rather than promotional.

## Narration

AntiTrade turns scattered trading work into one connected workspace.

Scan cross-asset markets and live intelligence without leaving your desk.

Ask the assistant to analyze your own journal, then keep watch with scheduled reports and risk alerts.

Review every trade through clean analytics, build reusable strategies, and validate ideas with backtests.

Finally, see your whole portfolio in context.

AntiTrade helps independent traders observe, plan, test, record, and improve.

Your process. One trading bench.
